## How I got here

My first real dev work was forced on me. In my fourth-year capstone I had to wrestle with environment setups and `localhost` and a pile of things I barely understood. Tutorials, a patient classmate, and a helpful professor got me through it, and somewhere along the way I realized I was enjoying it.

I've worked that way ever since. Pick something slightly out of reach, start before I'm ready, and ship the ugly version first.

> *"I am always doing what I cannot do yet, in order to learn how to do it."* — Vincent van Gogh

## Things I care about

- **Motion with a reason.** My own logo is one rigid wing rotated about a hinge, not a morph between two shapes. I like animation that has real geometry behind it.
- **Interfaces that feel fast**, not just benchmark fast.
- **Small, sharp tools** over sprawling apps.
- **Craft you can't see:** spacing, easing curves, the state you didn't forget to handle.
